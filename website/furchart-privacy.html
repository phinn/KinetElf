FurChart 隐私政策

生效日期:2026-09-29 · 适用于 iOS / macOS 应用「FurChart」

一句话总结

我们不收集你的任何数据。宠物档案、医疗记录、提醒全部只存在你自己的设备和你的 iCloud 私有数据库里;开发者没有任何服务器,不经手、不存储、不中转你的任何内容。

1. 我们收集的数据

无。FurChart 不含任何统计 SDK、广告 SDK、追踪器;不申请追踪权限;隐私清单(Privacy Manifest)中申报为零收集、零追踪域名。

2. 数据存储在哪

  • 设备本地:宠物档案、医疗记录、提醒等全部数据存储在你的设备沙盒目录(SQLite 本地库)。
  • iCloud 私有库:若你开启 iCloud,数据通过 Apple CloudKit 在你登录的同一 Apple ID 的设备间同步。这是你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库,开发者无法访问。同步使用 Apple 的加密传输与存储。
  • 照片:若你为记录添加照片,仅保存在本地/iCloud 私有库,不上传到任何第三方。

3. AI 拍照识别功能(如使用)

FurChart 支持「拍照识别录入」:将照片发给你自己配置的 AI 服务(自填 API Key 与端点,如 OpenAI 兼容接口)以抽取疫苗本/病历上的结构化信息。此功能:

  • 默认关闭,不配置则完全不可用,App 全部功能不依赖它;
  • 请求直接从你的设备发往你指定的服务商,不经过开发者任何服务器;
  • 你发送的照片与内容受你所选服务商的隐私政策约束;
  • 你的 API Key 仅保存在设备本地,不会随数据导出。

4. 权限使用

  • 通知:仅在你想接收疫苗/驱虫到期提醒时使用,可在系统设置关闭。
  • 相机/相册:仅在你想为记录拍照或添加照片时使用,拒绝授权不影响其他功能。
  • 不申请定位、通讯录、麦克风等任何其他权限。

5. 数据导出与删除

  • 你随时可将全部记录导出为 JSON 备份或 PDF 病历册,数据完全由你掌控。
  • 删除 App 即删除设备本地全部数据;iCloud 私有库数据可随时全量删除。

6. 儿童

FurChart 不面向儿童收集任何信息。

7. 政策变更

若政策发生重大变更,将在本页面更新并标注生效日期。

8. 联系方式

如有任何隐私相关问题,联系:phinn@qq.com

FurChart Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-09-29 · Applies to the iOS / macOS app "FurChart"

In one sentence

We collect nothing. Pet profiles, medical records, and reminders live only on your devices and in your own iCloud private database. We run no servers — we never see, store, or relay your data.

1. Data we collect

None. FurChart contains no analytics SDKs, no ad SDKs, no trackers. It never requests tracking permission. Our Privacy Manifest declares zero collected data types and zero tracking domains.

2. Where your data lives

  • On device: all data is stored locally in the app sandbox (SQLite).
  • iCloud (private DB): if enabled, data syncs across your devices signed into the same Apple ID via Apple CloudKit. This is your own private iCloud database — the developer cannot access it. Sync uses Apple's encrypted transport and storage.
  • Photos: photos you attach are stored locally / in your iCloud private DB only. No third-party upload.

3. AI photo capture (optional)

FurChart can extract structured fields from vaccine booklets / medical records using an AI service you configure (your own API key and endpoint, OpenAI-compatible). This feature:

  • is off by default — the entire app works fully without it;
  • sends requests directly from your device to the provider you chose; nothing passes through the developer;
  • is subject to the privacy policy of the provider you select;
  • stores your API key only on-device; it is never exported with your data.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications: only if you want vaccine/deworming reminders. Disable anytime in Settings.
  • Camera / Photo Library: only when you add photos to records. Denying does not affect other features.
  • No location, contacts, microphone, or any other permission is requested.

5. Export & deletion

  • Export everything as a JSON backup or a PDF record booklet anytime. Your data, your control.
  • Deleting the app removes all local data; iCloud private-DB data can be wiped anytime.

6. Children

FurChart collects no information from children.

7. Changes

Material changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: phinn@qq.com
